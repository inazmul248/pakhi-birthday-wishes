My sweetest Pakhi, Happy Birthday, my love. ♡ I wish I could sit beside you today and tell you all the things my heart keeps feeling when I think about you. On your birthday, I just want you to know how proud I am of you. I want you to know how much I love the version of you that is gentle, strong, patient, beautiful, and deeply caring. You deserve happiness that is not just temporary, but deep and lasting. And even though I may not be perfect, I hope you know that I will keep trying to be the kind of man who deserves your trust and your love. Happy Birthday, Pakhi. May this year bring you comfort, confidence, success, beautiful memories, and the kind of love that feels like home. May your smile always stay bright, your heart stay strong, and your life be filled with reasons to feel proud of yourself. You are loved. You are cherished. You are deeply, deeply special to me.
With all my love, Nazmul — Nazu ♡
